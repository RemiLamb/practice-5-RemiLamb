Serves 4 · 45 minutes

Slow BBQ Chicken

Low heat, a lid, and sauce added late so it never burns.

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken thighs bone in
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp of kosher salt
  • 1 tsp of black pepper
  • 1 tsp of garlic powder
  • 1 cup of barbeque sauce
  • 2 tbsp of cider vinegar
  • 1 tbsp of olive oil

Method

  1. Heat the Oven
  2. Season the Chicken
  3. Bake Covered
  4. Sauce and finish
  5. Rest and serve
1

Heat the Oven

Set it to 325 degrees. Low and Slow is the whole trick.
